# Your Steady plan

You only need to remember two things.

1. **Morning:** take the reading.
2. **Evening:** tap how the day was.

Everything else is optional. Missing a day is fine.

## Every morning (about 3 minutes)

1. Wake up. Go to the toilet if you need to.
2. Wet the strap. Put it on.
3. Lie back down.
4. Open Steady. Tap **Start morning reading**.
5. Lie still. Breathe normally.
6. **Two beeps** = stand up. Stand still.
7. **Long beep** = done.

Try to do it the same way each day. Same time, same bed, before coffee and before your phone.

**Bad morning?** Skip it. Just do the evening tap.

**Why:** your heart shows strain before you feel it. These readings show your normal, so Steady can spot when something changes.

## Every evening (1 tap)

A notification asks **How was today?**

- **Good**: a good day for you.
- **Okay**: a normal day for you.
- **Bad**: worse than normal, but not a crash.
- **Crash**: a crash, or PEM. If you'd call it a crash, tap Crash.

Missed it? Steady asks about yesterday the next morning.

**Why:** your taps teach Steady what a crash looks like for you. **Crash taps matter most.**

## During the day

Nothing to do. Keep your phone in your pocket when you can, so it counts steps.

Pace the way you normally do. Don't change anything because of the numbers. Steady isn't giving advice yet.

## How long

**4 weeks**, starting the first morning you take a reading.

- **Week 1:** get used to it.
- **Weeks 1–2:** Steady learns your normal. That takes 14 good mornings.
- **Weeks 3–4:** keep going. Log any crashes.

## Check-ins

Steady shows a card on the home screen when a check-in is due. You don't need to remember them.

1. **After 3 mornings: is it working?**
   Does the strap connect? Does the evening notification arrive? Is anything confusing or tiring?
2. **When Steady says "Your normal is ready"** (about 2 weeks in).
   We look at your trends together.
3. **After 4 weeks: what next?**
   We decide on the next step: warnings and an all-day device.

**How to check in:**

1. In Steady: **Settings → Export data**. Save the files to your computer.
2. Put them in the folder `steady/my-data/`. That folder is never uploaded.
3. Open Claude Code in the `steady` folder.
4. Type: **Steady check-in**

**Check in sooner if:** something breaks, a reading keeps failing, or the routine is costing you too much energy. Then make it simpler: skip the standing part (Settings), or do the reading only on good mornings.

## If something goes wrong

- **Strap not found:** wet it again and check it's snug. Close Polar Flow if it's open.
- **"Too noisy":** wet the strap and try again. Or skip today.
- **No evening notification:** in Android Settings, give Steady **Autostart** and set battery to **No restrictions**.

## Updates

When there's a new version, a card appears on the home screen. Tap **Update**.
