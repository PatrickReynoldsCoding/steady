# Phone test: Steady 0.1.0

On the POCO F2 Pro with the Polar H10.

1. **Install.** Open `Steady-0.1.0.apk` from Downloads and allow the install.
2. **Find the strap.** Wet the H10 electrodes and put it on. In Steady: *Find my strap*, allow
   *Nearby devices*. The H10 should appear as "Polar H10 XXXXXXXX". Tap it.
   - Not listed? Check Polar Flow (or another app) isn't connected to the strap.
3. **Steps.** On the home screen, *Count your steps?* → *Allow*.
4. **Reading.** Lie down, *Start morning reading*.
   - You should hear a beep within a few seconds of it finding the strap.
   - The lying countdown shows a heart rate.
   - Two beeps and a buzz at "Stand up now".
   - A longer beep at the end. Values look sensible (HRV typically 15–100 ms).
   - Try with the screen dimmed / the phone face down: the reading should continue.
5. **Too noisy.** Take a reading with a dry strap. It should say "too noisy" and offer *Try again*.
6. **Rating.** Settings → *Show the evening rating now*. Pull down notifications, tap a button.
   A "Saved" toast appears and the home screen shows "Today: …".
7. **Evening reminder.** Leave it on at 20:00 and check it arrives tonight. Xiaomi may block it.
   If it doesn't arrive, turn on *Autostart* for Steady and set Battery saver to *No restrictions*.
8. **Export.** Settings → *Export data*. Save to Drive or send to yourself. Three CSV files.
